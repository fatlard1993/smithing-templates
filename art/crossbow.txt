# Trim overlays for the crossbow.
# W H M D are the shades of vanilla's trim_base palette, lightest to darkest; '.' is bare.
# Each block is one pattern: its name, then sixteen rows of sixteen.
# Each pattern borrows the motif of its sword drawing in sword.txt, fitted to the crossbow.
# The string and what is loaded cross the stock, and are redrawn as it is drawn and loaded, so
# every inlay keeps to the two arms of the bow and the butt of the stock, which never change.
