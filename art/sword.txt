# Trim overlays for the silhouette every vanilla sword shares.
# W H M D are the shades of vanilla's trim_base palette, lightest to darkest; '.' is bare.
# Each block is one pattern: its name, then sixteen rows of sixteen.
# A lightning stroke, jagged from edge to edge.
# A wave rolling from edge to edge down the blade.
# Dashes running down the fuller, like ripples in sand.
# An eye at the base of the blade, pupil dark.
# A swirl on the blade, open in the middle.
# A wrapped grip and a capped pommel.
# Raised arms: a V opening up from the guard.
# Ribs: bars across the blade.
# A plain band across the blade and caps on the guard: the watchman's belt.
# A frame drawn round the blade's edge.
# Ornate: spine, crossbar, guard caps and pommel all inlaid.
# Two nostrils side by side, and a ring on the pommel.
# A lit tip, tapering into the fuller.
# Fins along the trailing edge, like a guardian's back.
# Wings: both arms of the guard.
# The warden's solid spine: the whole fuller lit.
# Waymarks on alternate edges, a route traced along the blade.
# A vine climbing the blade with leaves at the guard.
