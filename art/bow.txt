# Trim overlays for the bow.
# W H M D are the shades of vanilla's trim_base palette, lightest to darkest; '.' is bare.
# Each block is one pattern: its name, then sixteen rows of sixteen.
# Each pattern borrows the motif of its sword drawing in sword.txt, fitted to the bow.
# The bow redraws its limbs' tips and its string as it is drawn, so every inlay keeps to the
# middle of the limbs and the grip, which stay the same through the whole draw.
